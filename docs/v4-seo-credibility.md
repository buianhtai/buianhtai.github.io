# V4 — SEO, credibility, and site quality

## What this adds

- Localized author pages at `/en/about/` and `/vi/about/`, linked from navigation, homepage and footer. Personal biography avoids unverifiable employment claims and private project details.
- Real GitHub Pages `/404.html` with `noindex,follow` and links to the archive and home.
- `/robots.txt` linking to the existing Astro sitemap index. `@astrojs/sitemap` continues to own sitemap generation.
- Canonical URL and description, Open Graph/Twitter card, locale metadata, real page-specific PNG preview, and article publication/update timestamps.
- `hreflang` alternates only for top-level pages that actually exist in both languages. No invented translated equivalents for articles.
- `WebSite` JSON-LD on pages, `Person` JSON-LD on the About page, and existing `BlogPosting` schema on standard articles.
- Automatic, build-time 1200 × 630 PNG cards for all published MDX posts without custom hero assets. Generated by Sharp via Astro static endpoints. Custom hero images take precedence.
- Accessible Foundations reading markup: eliminate nested `main` landmarks.
- Lighthouse CI on EN homepage, EN About and EN blog, with saved HTML and JSON reports.

## Manual launch checklist

1. **Merge the V4 PR only after both Validate Astro build and Lighthouse Quality Audit workflows have passed.**
2. Open `/en/about/`, `/vi/about/`, `/404.html`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap-index.xml`, and `/og/site.png`. Confirm the language switch stays on About.
3. Test an article without an explicit hero image: `/en/blog/adapter-facade-patterns` should advertise `https://buianhtai.github.io/og/en/adapter-facade-patterns.png` as its `og:image`; also test one with a custom image.
4. Inspect page source for canonical, `hreflang` and `noindex` metadata; use [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) where applicable.
5. **Google Search Console ownership verification requires your own Google account action.** Add a URL-prefix property for `https://buianhtai.github.io/` and choose an available verification method, then submit `https://buianhtai.github.io/sitemap-index.xml`. Do not commit Google verification tokens or secrets unless you deliberately choose the HTML-file verification method.
6. Review the uploaded `v4-lighthouse-audits` artifact. The first Lighthouse CI scores are baselines; warning thresholds are intentionally nonblocking until you decide on achievable budgets.

## Build and QA

`npm ci && npm run build` produces `dist/404.html`, `dist/robots.txt`, `dist/sitemap-index.xml`, `dist/og/site.png`, `dist/og/{lang}/{slug}.png`, localized About pages, and the Pagefind index.

Lighthouse runs on a local copy of `dist` using `lighthouserc.json`. It writes reports to `artifacts/lighthouse/` and does not send them to public third-party storage.
