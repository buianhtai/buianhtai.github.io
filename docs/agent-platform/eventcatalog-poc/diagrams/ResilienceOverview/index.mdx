# Reliability, Recovery and Scaling Map

**Status: proposed design.** Fictional, domain-neutral, and implementation-neutral; not evidence of deployed cloud infrastructure or an approved technology stack.

## How to read it

Workers may crash, queues may redeliver, and remote writes may complete after their acknowledgments are lost. Never claim exactly-once side effects. Apply fencing to terminal commits, bounded retries to safe tasks, reconciliation to ambiguous remote effects, and tenant fairness to scheduling. Observe queue age, lease expiry, cost, tool denials, pending approvals and unreconciled outcomes.
