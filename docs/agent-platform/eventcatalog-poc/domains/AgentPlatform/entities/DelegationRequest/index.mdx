## Lifecycle and invariants

A parent may call only approved subagent aliases; the child receives a deliberately narrow task/context and never unrestricted parent credentials. Child rights are the intersection of the parent delegation, child grants, principal and resource ACL. Budgets, maximum depth, cancellation and failures propagate through typed run results.

## Fictional example

A research coordinator delegates evidence lookup to a read-only specialist, waits for its result and publishes a combined answer with provenance.
