## 1. What the winners actually do (evidence, not vibes)

### Refactoring — dissected post: "How to make AI better at product" (~2,800 words, free monthly essay)

| Element | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Opening frame | "Hey, Luca here! Welcome to a ✨ monthly free essay ✨" — 2 lines, always identical |
| Hook | A trending artifact (viral quote + screenshot from X) within the first 3 paragraphs |
| Stance | One opinionated sentence: "I'll go out and say I like this direction" |
| Gap | What the current conversation misses — the reason this post exists |
| Agenda | 4-bullet preview with emoji-titled sections before "Let's dive in!" |
| Sections | Bold-claim headings ("Why product is harder than coding"); paragraphs of 1–3 sentences; bold key phrases everywhere |
| Evidence | Concrete numbers in every section (137 ADRs, 9% vs 90%, rework 60%→20%) + screenshots as proof |
| Links | 5–8 internal links to past issues (creates a web, boosts archive) |
| Close | Continuity tease ("Will let you know how it works!") + "Sincerely 👋 Luca" |

## 2. The Nodes & Edges post template

Apply to every edition before shipping:

```text
1. FRAME (2 lines, fixed)
   "Hey, Tai here! ..." — what Nodes & Edges is + why this issue exists.

2. HOOK (2–3 paragraphs)
   A trending quote, an incident, or your own war story — WITH an artifact
   (screenshot, error message, diagram). Never open with definitions.

3. STANCE (1 paragraph)
   One opinionated sentence people could disagree with. This is what gets
   shared. No stance = no post.

4. AGENDA (3–4 bullets)
   Emoji-titled section previews. Ends with "Let's dive in!"

5. SECTIONS ×3–4
   • Heading = bold claim, not a label ("Why X is harder than Y")
   • Paragraphs of 1–3 sentences
   • At least one concrete number per section
   • One embedded architecture diagram per post (our differentiator)
   • 1–2 internal links to past issues

6. CLOSE
   Continuity tease for next issue + sincere sign-off.
```

### Voice rules (anti-AI-slop)

- First person, always. "I" statements over passive constructions.
- One uncomfortable take per post — say the thing colleagues won't.
- Numbers beat adjectives: "137 ADRs" not "lots of decisions".
- Short paragraphs. If a paragraph hits 4 sentences, split it.
- Contractions, asides, occasional one-line paragraphs for emphasis.
- Never: "In today's rapidly evolving landscape", "delve", "comprehensive", "It's important to note".

## 3. Cadence & pricing (market-calibrated)

| Decision | Value | Basis |

| Paywall trigger | ~5K subs OR when backlog of paid-only ideas exceeds 6 | Refactoring ran free for years first |

## 4. Revision checklist — apply to every edition

- [ ] Frame + hook present? (No definition openings)
- [ ] One opinionated stance stated plainly?
- [ ] Agenda bullets before the dive?
- [ ] ≥1 number per major section?
- [ ] ≥1 embedded diagram?
- [ ] 2+ internal links to past issues?
- [ ] Continuity tease + sign-off?
- [ ] Read aloud test: does any sentence sound like a chatbot wrote it? Rewrite it.
